What Are {{TOPIC_NAME}}?

A {{TOPIC_LOWERCASE}} represents a part of a whole. The word "whole" means a complete unit or object that can be divided into equal parts. For example, a whole pizza, a whole hour, or a whole dollar.

A {{TOPIC_LOWERCASE}} consists of two numbers separated by a line:

3
4
↑ NUMERATOR
(parts you have)
↓ DENOMINATOR
(total equal parts)
  • Numerator (top number): Tells you how many parts you have
  • Denominator (bottom number): Tells you how many equal parts make up one whole

Example: In the {{TOPIC_LOWERCASE}} 3/4, you have 3 parts out of 4 equal parts that make up the whole.
