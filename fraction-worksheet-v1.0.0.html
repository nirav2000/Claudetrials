Interactive Fraction Comparison Worksheet

How to Use:

  • Keyboard: Type <, >, or = directly. Shortcuts: 'l' or ',' for <, 'g' or '.' for >, '-' for =
  • Buttons: Click the symbol buttons to select your answer
  • Drawing: Draw the symbol on the canvas, then click "Done" to recognize it. Click "Clear" to start over
  • Voice: Click the microphone icon and say "less than", "greater than", or "equal" (Chrome/Edge only)
